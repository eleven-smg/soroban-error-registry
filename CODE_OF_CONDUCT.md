# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Assume the other person is acting in good faith. Review the change,
not the person who wrote it.

## Expected behaviour

- Give specific, actionable feedback on issues and pull requests.
- Accept that maintainers may decline a change, and that contributors may
  disagree with a review.
- Respect that people here work across many time zones and languages, and that
  a slow reply is not a slight.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Personal attacks, harassment, or discriminatory language of any kind.
- Publishing anyone's private information.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, or repeatedly reopening settled
  decisions without new information.
- Opening low-effort or automated pull requests purely to inflate contribution
  counts.

## Enforcement

Report a problem by opening a private security advisory or by contacting the
maintainer through their GitHub profile. Reports are handled confidentially.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close issues and pull requests, or
block accounts that do not follow this document. Serious or repeated breaches
result in a permanent block.
